Before You Ride

Safety Briefing

Watch our quick safety overview before booking. It only takes a minute.

Final Step

Review & Book

Read and agree to our liability waiver, then proceed to booking.

1. Assumption of Risk

Participant voluntarily assumes all risks associated with the party bus experience, including but not limited to personal injury, property damage, or loss arising from participation in activities aboard the vehicle.

2. Release of Liability

Participant releases Most Litt Party Rides, its owners, employees, and agents from any and all liability, claims, or causes of action arising out of the use of the party bus service.

3. Alcohol Policy

Passengers consuming alcohol must be 21 years of age or older and present valid ID upon request. The driver reserves the right to refuse service to anyone visibly intoxicated. No illegal substances permitted.

4. Conduct

Participants agree to behave respectfully toward the driver, other passengers, and the vehicle. Damage to the vehicle or its equipment will result in full repair charges billed to the booking party.

5. Capacity & Safety

Maximum passenger capacity must not be exceeded. Passengers must remain seated while the vehicle is in motion on highways. Standing is permitted at low speeds in designated areas only.

6. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made less than 48 hours prior to the scheduled booking are non-refundable. Rescheduling is available with 72 hours' notice subject to availability.

7. Smoking Policy

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the vehicle at all times. A cleaning fee of $150 will be assessed for violations.

8. Damage & Cleaning

Any excessive mess, vomit, or damage caused by passengers will incur additional cleaning or repair fees charged to the credit card on file at the time of booking.

9. Route & Stops

All routes and stops must be agreed upon prior to departure. Significant deviations from the agreed route may incur additional charges at the driver's discretion.

10. Indemnification

Participant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Most Litt Party Rides from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from participant's actions during the rental period.
