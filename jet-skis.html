Liability Waiver

Read the full waiver below and confirm your agreement before booking.

1. Assumption of Water Sport Risks

I acknowledge that jet ski operation involves inherent risks including collisions with other watercraft, submerged objects, capsizing, strong currents, and personal injury or death. I voluntarily assume all such risks.

2. Release of Liability

I hereby release, waive, and discharge Most Litt Party Rides, its owners, employees, and agents from all claims arising from bodily injury, death, or property damage during the jet ski rental, even if caused by negligence.

3. Operating Requirements

I certify I am at least 16 years old to operate solo (8 years as a passenger), possess a valid ID, have not consumed alcohol or controlled substances within 8 hours, am in adequate physical condition, and have received and understood the mandatory safety briefing.

4. Mandatory Safety Equipment

I agree to wear a Coast Guard-approved life jacket at all times on the water and to use the engine cut-off lanyard (kill switch) when operating. Failure to comply results in immediate termination of rental with no refund.

5. Operating Rules

I agree to obey all applicable laws, maintain safe speed and distance from other watercraft and swimmers, stay within the designated operating area, and follow all instructions from the certified water safety guide at all times.

6. Alcohol & Substance Policy

Operating under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly prohibited. Any impaired operator will be removed from the water immediately with no refund. The Company reserves the right to perform a sobriety assessment.

7. Equipment Damage

I accept full financial responsibility for any damage to the jet ski or equipment caused by misuse, negligence, or violation of operating rules during my rental period.

8. Weather & Conditions

The Company may cancel or suspend rentals due to inclement weather or unsafe conditions. Company-initiated cancellations receive a full refund or reschedule option. No refunds after the rental period begins.

9. Indemnification

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless Most Litt Party Rides from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from my use of the jet ski, my violation of any law, or harm caused to third parties.

10. Acknowledgment

I have read this waiver, fully understand its terms, and sign freely and voluntarily. I am giving up substantial rights. This waiver is governed by the laws of North Carolina.
